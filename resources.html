Design Craft Digital & Hardcover

Refactoring UI

The most pragmatic guide to designing clean, high-conversion interfaces from a developer and designer perspective.

Key insight: Design with visual hierarchy and intentional spacing before decorative chrome.

Product Strategy Classic Hardcover

The Design of Everyday Things

Don Norman

The definitive bible on cognitive ergonomics, affordances, signifiers, and designing products that humans intuitively understand.

Key insight: When users fail, it is not user error — it is faulty interface design.

Design Craft Reference Book

The Elements of Typographic Style

Robert Bringhurst

Widely revered as the definitive text on typographic craft, rhythm, proportional scales, and the architecture of the printed page.

Key insight: Typography exists to honor content and guide human comprehension.

Systems & Tech Technical Book

A Philosophy of Software Design

John Ousterhout

A masterclass on managing system complexity, modular interfaces, and why shallow modules ruin large systems.

Key insight: Modules should be deep: simple interfaces hiding profound internal power.

Design Craft Visual Reference

Universal Principles of Design

William Lidwell et al.

An authoritative encyclopedia of 125 cognitive, perceptual, and structural laws governing human interaction.

Key insight: Master Fitts' Law, Hick's Law, and progressive disclosure to reduce cognitive load.

Product Strategy Product Methodology

Shape Up

Ryan Singer (Basecamp)

A revolutionary framework for product teams: fixed time, variable scope, 6-week cycles, and building with shaped appetites.

Key insight: Never start building until problems are shaped with clear boundaries.

Product Strategy Management Guide

Inspired: How to Create Tech Products

Marty Cagan

The gold standard for product discovery, continuous customer validation, and building empowered product teams.

Key insight: Validate value, usability, feasibility, and viability before writing code.

Systems & Tech Engineering Architecture

Designing Data-Intensive Applications

Martin Kleppmann

The definitive exploration of storage engines, distributed consensus, data models, and scalable systems reliability.

Key insight: A designer who understands backend latency and data models designs 10x better products.

Design Craft Classic Monograph

Grid Systems in Graphic Design

Josef Müller-Brockmann

The Swiss design masterwork outlining mathematical grid systems for layout, typography, and architectural coherence.

Key insight: The grid is a structural aid, not a straightjacket — it creates rhythm and order.
